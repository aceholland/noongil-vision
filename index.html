About Noongil

Noongil is an assistive vision system designed to support visually impaired and low-vision individuals in understanding their immediate surroundings. Using computer vision models directly on your device, it detects objects, tracks movement, and calculates approximate distance in real time.

Identified objects are communicated through spoken voice feedback, alerting users to nearby obstacles and changes in their path. The system operates fully within the browser, ensuring continuous spatial assistance with complete privacy and zero data transmission.

Voice Direction: Spoken feedback announces approaching obstacles and estimated distances.
Distance Estimation: Continuous focal calculation helps gauge how close surrounding objects are.
Local Execution: Camera processing runs locally on your browser without recording or uploading video.
